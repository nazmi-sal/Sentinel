### **SECURITY PROJECT**



# Database Design: Sentinel

|**Entity**|**Key attributes**|**Relationships**|
|-|-|-|
|User|user\_id (PK), user\_name, email, role, password|> Reports many Incidents <br />> Investigates many Incidents<br />> Performs many StatusChanges|
|Incident|incident\_id (PK), title, category, severity, description, status, created\_at, closed\_at|> Reported by one User <br />> Assigned to one User (Investigator) <br />> Has many Evidence<br />> Has many StatusChanges<br />> Has one SummaryReport|
|StatusChange (audit trail)|status\_change\_id (PK), old\_status, new\_status, changed\_at|> Belongs to one Incident<br />> Performed by one User|
|Evidence|evidence\_id (PK), file\_name, file\_type, file\_path/URL, notes, uploaded\_at|> Evidence<br />Belongs to one Incident<br />> Uploaded by one User|
|SummaryReport|report\_id (PK), generated\_at, summary\_text|> Belongs to one Incident<br />> Generated by one User|
